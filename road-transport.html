FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about road transportation services

What information is needed for a road freight quote?

Please provide pickup and delivery locations, cargo description, package count, dimensions, total weight and cargo-ready date.

Can every listed destination be served by road?

Not necessarily. Road feasibility depends on the specific route, border and transit requirements, cargo type and operating arrangements. Each request is checked individually.

Do you arrange cross-border documentation?

Tell us what cargo you are shipping and the route. Any documentation or customs-related scope must be confirmed as part of the quotation; do not assume it is included unless stated.

When is delivery expected?

An estimated schedule can be discussed after the route and shipment details have been assessed. It is not a guaranteed delivery date.
