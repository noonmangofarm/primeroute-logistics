FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about sea freight services

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL generally means a shipment uses a full container, while LCL means cargo shares container space with other shipments. The suitable option depends on cargo volume, route and handling.

What details should I provide?

Share the cargo description, package count, dimensions, total weight, pickup location, destination and cargo-ready date. Provide any known packing or documentation requirements.

Which countries can I enquire about?

The company lists Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Russia, Kuwait, Bahrain and Canada as destination markets. The availability of sea freight for a particular route must be confirmed.

Are sailing dates and transit times guaranteed?

No. Schedules and estimates depend on carrier availability, route, cargo acceptance, port operations and other factors. They are confirmed during quotation.
