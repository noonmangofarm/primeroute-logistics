FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about air freight services

What information is needed for an air freight quotation?

Please provide the cargo description, number of packages, weight, dimensions, pickup location, destination and cargo-ready date. Include any special handling or documentation requirements.

Can you confirm delivery time before I submit an enquiry?

Transit estimates depend on the route, carrier, cargo acceptance and handling requirements. The proposed schedule must be confirmed during the quotation process.

Which destinations can I ask about?

Prime Route Logistics lists Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Russia, Kuwait, Bahrain and Canada as its destination markets. Air freight availability is assessed for the specific route and shipment.

Does submitting the form confirm a booking?

No. The form starts an enquiry. A booking is only confirmed after the service, routing, charges, cargo acceptance and other applicable details have been agreed.
