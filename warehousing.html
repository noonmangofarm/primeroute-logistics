FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about warehousing and storage services

What information is needed for a storage quotation?

Provide the cargo description, quantity, dimensions or volume, expected storage period, required start date and any special handling or storage conditions.

Can I request handling as well as storage?

Include any receiving, packing, inventory handling or dispatch requirements in your enquiry. The available scope is confirmed before quotation.

Is warehousing available for every type of cargo?

Cargo acceptance depends on the goods, storage conditions, facility capability, applicable rules and operational approval. Describe your cargo accurately so it can be assessed.

Can storage be combined with freight services?

You can ask for storage alongside a freight enquiry. The availability and scope of combined arrangements must be confirmed for your shipment.
